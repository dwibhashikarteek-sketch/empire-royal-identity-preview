Honouring food with gratitude

“अन्नं ब्रह्मा रसो विष्णुः भोजना देवता स्मृतः।
भुक्ते भक्त्या ततः पश्चात् त्रिप्तिः प्रजायते॥”

“Annam Brahma raso Vishnu, bhojana devata smritah.
Bhukte bhaktya tatah pashchat triptih prajayate.”

Translation: Food is Brahma (the creator), taste is Vishnu (the sustainer), and digestion/eating is a divine process. When food is consumed with devotion, it leads to true satisfaction and nourishment.

Before you begin

A few useful
answers.

Can I use this on my phone?

This website uses a responsive layout. Open the client intake form in your phone’s browser. Physical Android and iPhone testing remains limited.

How accurate are the calorie values?

They are editorial approximate estimates, not laboratory measurements or verified recipe calculations. Use cooked portion weights and review ingredients. A clinician should assess any plan before it is used for individual care.

Where do my intake answers go?

The intake form stores your submitted answers in Google Forms under SRI VENKATESWARA EMPIRE CLINIC. Health questions are optional. Other respondents cannot see response summaries. Contact the clinic for correction or deletion requests.

Does submitting the form book a consultation?

No. Please contact the clinic through your usual channel to arrange an appointment. The form helps prepare for a consultation and is not monitored for emergencies.
